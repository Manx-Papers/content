**This is a test post** for checking clippings with a screen reader and on a phone. It is dated
1 June 2027, so the Stories list doesn't show it before then; it opens only by its direct link.
Every clipping of the wedding comes from [one page of the Isle of Man Times](/read/IMT/IMT-1954-04-24/6),
24 April 1954. At the end are two more checks: two clippings with the same headline, and a
photograph with the paper's own printed caption.

In April 1954 [the Isle of Man Times reported](/items/IMT/IMT-1954-04-24/0006/record/100) the
wedding of a Clubman's T.T. rider.

![Clubman's T.T. rider weds](section:IMT/IMT-1954-04-24/0006/block/R00100-B000?highlight=rider+weds "The headline, with two words highlighted.")

The paper printed a photograph of the wedding group.

![The wedding group](section:IMT/IMT-1954-04-24/0006/block/R00101-B000 "A photograph: no text to read, so it has a description.")
::: description
A wedding group of about twenty people stands in front of a stone building. In the centre the
bride, in a veil and a long white dress, holds a large bouquet, with the bridegroom beside her in a
dark suit. Bridesmaids in short-sleeved dresses with bouquets stand at either side, and family and
guests crowd behind.
:::

The groom was [Gwilym Owen](/items/IMT/IMT-1954-04-24/0006/record/100), only son of Mr and Mrs R.
Owen of Brunswick Road, and the wedding was at Kirk Braddan.

![Gwilym Owen marries at Kirk Braddan](section:IMT/IMT-1954-04-24/0006/block/R00100-B001?highlight=married+Kirk "The report's first paragraph. The machine read “Clubman” as “ClYubman”.")

The report goes on in the next column, where [the paper names](/items/IMT/IMT-1954-04-24/0006/record/245)
the minister and describes the bride's gown.

![The Rev. B. Kelly officiated](section:IMT/IMT-1954-04-24/0006/block/R00245-B000?highlight="Rev.+B.+Kelly" "A phrase highlighted.")

![The bridesmaids](section:IMT/IMT-1954-04-24/0006/block/R00246-B000?highlight=Joan+Jean "The groom's twin sisters were the bridesmaids.")

![The reception](section:IMT/IMT-1954-04-24/0006/block/R00247-B000 "The author's corrected text replaces the machine's.")
::: text
rocks and B. Binns the groomsmen.

After the ceremony the bride's mother gave a reception for the happy couple at "Ramona,"
Woodville Terrace. The bridegroom is in the Royal Artillery.
:::

## What to check

- Each clipping is a link that opens the whole Article.
- The alt text names the paper, the date and any highlighted words.
- "Read the text" opens with the highlighted words marked; the last clipping shows the author's
  corrected text, and the photograph shows a description.
- The two clippings headlined "The wedding report" are told apart: their links are named "The
  wedding report (1)" and "(2)", on the clipping itself and on its "Full page" link.
- The Harbour Master photograph shows "Printed caption:" with the paper's own caption, then
  "(read by machine)". It has no description, so it has no "Read the text".
- The wedding clippings all come from one page, so that page image should load once.
